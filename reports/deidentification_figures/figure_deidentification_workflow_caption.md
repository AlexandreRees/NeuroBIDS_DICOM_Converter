**Figure 1 | De-identification workflow.**  
Raw DICOM acquisitions are retained as an immutable archive and are accessed only in a read-only manner for inventory and participant–session mapping. Automated de-identification is applied exclusively to derived copies and comprises removal of protected health information from designated header fields, participant-specific date shifting, deterministic unique-identifier remapping, and removal of private tags. Pixel data are not altered. Downstream DICOM-to-NIfTI conversion, BIDS organization, and quality control operate on the de-identified repository, preserving provenance while leaving original acquisitions untouched.
