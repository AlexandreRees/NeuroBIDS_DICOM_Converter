**Figure 2 | Summary of DICOM metadata transformations during de-identification.**  
Bar heights indicate the number of explicitly catalogued DICOM fields (or policy categories) subject to each transformation class in the documented confidentiality policy. Most identifier and free-text fields were cleared; patient name and patient ID were pseudonymized to mapped study codes; selected unique identifiers were remapped; calendar dates were shifted by a participant-specific offset; and acquisition-relevant fields were preserved. Removal of all private tags and retention of unaltered pixel data are each represented as a single policy category. Counts summarize the policy table rather than empirical prevalence of every tag in every series.
