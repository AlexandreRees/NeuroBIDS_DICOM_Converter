**Figure 3 | Illustrative before-and-after DICOM header transformation (synthetic example).**  
Left: representative identifier, date, and study unique-identifier fields as they may appear prior to de-identification. Right: corresponding fields after transformation, in which patient name and patient ID are replaced by a canonical study code, the study date is shifted by a fixed participant-specific offset, and the study instance unique identifier is remapped to a new value. No real participant information is shown. Absolute calendar dates are obscured while relative longitudinal intervals within a participant are preserved by using a constant offset per individual.
