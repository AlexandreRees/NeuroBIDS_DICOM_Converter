Functional MRI paradigm summary. All BOLD runs share TR 937 ms, TE 37 ms, flip angle 52°, 2 mm isotropic voxels, and multiband acceleration factor 8. Durations are approximate (volumes × TR). Stimulation was monocular; the non-stimulated eye was occluded.
